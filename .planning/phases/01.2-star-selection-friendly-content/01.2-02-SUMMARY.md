# Phase 1.2 Plan 02: v1.2 email render Summary

The email no longer has an image under the headline. Each card shows its own image, card 1 included, and a 💡 활용 아이디어 line inside the 3-line cell. 모델 정보 rows with `확인 필요` are hidden. ## What changed

- `email.html.j2` (template-version 5). The hero section keeps `data-section="hero"` with only the headline (D-07). `card(it)` lost `show_image` and renders `it.image` at the top of every card. The alt text is still "{title_ko} 대표 그림" (D-08). The 3-line cell ends with `<br>💡 {{ it.use_idea_ko }}`, which is autoescaped (D-22). The 모델 정보 table loops over `info_rows[it.rank]`, and `loop.last` runs on the filtered list. The table and its heading are skipped when the list is empty (D-10). The greeting uses the D-06 sentence. Both section titles take `basis_note` as the subtitle, and the rest title comes from `rest_title`.
- `email.txt.j2` (template-version 5) follows the same rules: a 💡 line, only the known info lines, and the `🧩 모델 정보` heading only when a row exists. It also has the D-06 greeting, `rest_title` and `basis_note`.
- `render.py` adds `_info_rows(it)`, `rest_title(n)` (None for N <= 3, "놓치기 아까운 4위 👀" for 4, "놓치기 아까운 4위부터 {N}위까지 👀" for 5 to 10) and `basis_note(issue)`. The `hero_image` key is gone. `MAX_EMAIL_BYTES = 90 * 1024` is unchanged.
- `test_render.py` has 59 tests, and fixture texts are read rather than hard-coded. The 15 new or renamed tests match `-k "info_rows_hidden or star_greeting or rest_title or basis_note"`. The hero test now asserts no `<img>`. A new test checks that every image sits on its own card, with src, alt and size checked and the image placed before the meta line.

## Size budget (DSGN-08, T-01.2-06)

- Worst case: 10 card images on `raw.githubusercontent.com` at exactly 300 chars, no hero, `use_idea_ko = "활" * 50`, `github_stars = 99999`, every link, every 모델 정보 value known, and every Phase 3 slot filled.
- **Measured: 90,668 B. Cap: 92,160 B. Headroom: 1,492 B.** W44 fixture: 45,136 B.
- The research estimate was about 89.5 KB. The gap comes mostly from the 300-char raw URLs, about 190 B longer per card than the old cdn-uploads URLs used in that measurement.
- Research Pitfall 8 expected about 3 KB to be left for the Phase 2 verdict badges and Phase 3 slots. Only about 1.5 KB is left. Rendering the badge row on all 10 cards will probably go over the cap. Phase 2 must trim the repeated per-card markup first, for example the shared style strings or the per-cell font stack on the info rows, or it must shorten the badge markup.

## Known edge case (D-17)

A paper with a repo but a missing `githubStars` gets `github_stars = None`. It shows `▲ {upvotes}` and triggers the "(코드 없는 논문은 Hugging Face 추천 수)" subtitle even though it has code. Research found 0 such papers on the live W44 data, so this is documented and not handled. The W44 fixture has 4 items with `github_stars = None`, so its subtitle is the mixed one.
