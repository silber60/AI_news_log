## What changed

- **schema.py:** `SCHEMA_VERSION = 3`. The constants are `RAW_IMAGE_HOST`, `IMAGE_HOSTS` and `IMAGE_URL_MAX = 300`. The new `_require_image_host` allows the HF CDN and raw.githubusercontent.com, and rejects any URL with `?` or `#` or over 300 characters. `IMAGE_HOST` stays because enrich.py imports it. Also added: `Item.github_stars`, `Item.use_idea_ko` and `ItemAnalysisLLM.use_idea_ko`. `HeadlineLLM` and `HfPaper` are unchanged.
- **select.py:** `STAR_MIN = 100`, `N_MIN = 3` and `N_MAX = 10`. New functions are `has_repo`, `stars` (int only, not bool, at least 0), `star_key` (missing stars sort after 0) and `select_top(entries)`; the old `n` parameter is gone. The upvote `sort_key` is kept for the fill.
- **analyze.to_item / pipeline:** `github_stars` is `select.stars(entry)` for repo papers. Each selected.json item gets `github_stars` and `basis` (`"stars"` or `"upvotes"`).
- **Templates:** card meta reads `★ {:,}` when `github_stars` is set and `▲ upvotes` otherwise. This is the only template change.
- **W44 build order:** 2610.35518 (5,221), 40599, 36729, 31635, 30006, 32253, 33106, 14018, 33005, 33845. This matches must_haves. Rank 3 is Hy-Nomas. Its captures give release `가중치 · GitHub · 데모`; size and training data stay `확인 필요` because the capture has no safetensors or cardData datasets.

## Known edge case (D-17, documented, not changed)
