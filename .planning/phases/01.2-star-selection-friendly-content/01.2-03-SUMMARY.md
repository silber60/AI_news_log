### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Bracketed URLs left a stray "(" in the paper input**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** `_URL_RE = https?://\S+` swallowed the closing bracket, so "(https://x)" became "(" and the D-14 strip could not match.
- **Fix:** `_URL_RE = https?://[^\s()\[\]（）<>]*` stops at brackets (and matches a bare scheme), then `_EMPTY_BRACKETS` removes the leftover "()". No "http" text can survive.
- **Files modified:** newsletter/stages/analyze.py
- **Commit:** d866c5f

- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** when the only bad ending sat in a field that the length trim cut to "…", the recomputed field list was empty.

- **Commit:** 56d651f
