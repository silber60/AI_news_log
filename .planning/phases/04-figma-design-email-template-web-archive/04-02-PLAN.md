## Phase Goal

**As a** subscriber, **I want to** open any issue on the web from the email, browse past issues on one site, and leave the list with one confirmed click, **so that** the newsletter feels like a real brand I can read anywhere and stop whenever I like.

<objective>
Make the web issue page show what the email leaves out: the code-decided verdict reasons, both licenses and their evidence (D-05, Phase 2 D-02), and make it readable on a phone.

Purpose: ROADMAP SC5. The email stays lean; the web copy carries the proof.
Output: render.basis_rows / basis_links, web-only template block and responsive rules, tests, owner preview of one issue page.
</objective>
