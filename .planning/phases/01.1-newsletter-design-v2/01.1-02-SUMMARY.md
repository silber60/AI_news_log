## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] FASTOCTREE quantize made opaque pixels alpha 254**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** The palette alpha was bucketed, so no pixel was fully opaque. The plan's halo check (alpha 255 next to alpha 0) became vacuous and the otter bodies were slightly see-through.
- **Fix:** Snap palette alpha within 4 of 0/255 via `getpalette("RGBA")`/`putpalette`.
- **Files:** scripts/build_email_assets.py. **Commit:** 961fb81

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Flood-fill thresh 12 ate the wrench outline**
- **Found during:** Task 2, zoomed visual check
- **Issue:** The canvas is exactly (30,30,30), and the wrench's dark outline (about (33,35,41)) falls inside thresh 12, so parts of it were keyed out.
- **Fix:** `FLOOD_THRESH = 0` plus `MinFilter(3)` on the alpha mask to drop the 1 px anti-aliased fringe.
- **Files:** scripts/build_email_assets.py, the 3 otter PNGs (regenerated with `--force`; nothing has been pushed). **Commit:** 961fb81

**3. [Rule 1 - Test precision] Halo "dark" definition narrowed to neutral canvas color**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** With only "within 12 of 30", the tool otter's real dark wrench outline counted as halo (7/46 = 15%).
- **Fix:** A dark pixel also needs a channel spread of at most 3, because the canvas is neutral grey and the art's strokes are tinted. I added an edge-count guard so the test cannot pass vacuously. The negative control still flags the (30,30,30) ring.
- **Files:** tests/test_assets.py. **Commit:** 9f4f3d4
