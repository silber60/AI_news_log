## Phase Goal

**As a** reader who finds the site, **I want to** sign up with my name and email and get the latest issue right away, **so that** I can start reading this week instead of waiting for Monday, and every later Monday mail reaches me without the owner doing anything.

<objective>
Tracer slice of Phase 4.1: the welcome mail's content, rendered once by render.py and published as docs/welcome/latest.json, proven end to end by filling it the way Apps Script will and checking shape and size. Then the D-09 footer 구독하기 link and the weekly-bytes guard.

Purpose: D-04 makes render.py the one source of truth for the welcome HTML. Apps Script (04.1-02) only fills two slots and sends. If this payload is wrong, every welcome mail is wrong.
Output: render_welcome, the welcome template rows, latest.json from build_site, tests/test_welcome.py, the regenerated docs/welcome/latest.json.
</objective>
