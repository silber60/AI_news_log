**As a** reader who finds the site, **I want to** sign up with my name and email and get the latest issue right away, **so that** I can start reading this week instead of waiting for Monday, and every later Monday mail reaches me without the owner doing anything.

Make the weekly send read the live list from the Sheet, and add the one-time import of the current SUBSCRIBERS_CSV readers.

Purpose: SUBS-04. New sign-ups reach the Monday mail with no secret edit, and no address enters a log, an artifact or the repo.
Output: members.py, the deliver switch, importer.py and its CLI command, import-subscribers.yml, the Deliver env line, tests.
</objective>
