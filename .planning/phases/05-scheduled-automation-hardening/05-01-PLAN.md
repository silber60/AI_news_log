## Phase Goal

**As the** newsletter owner, **I want** the issue to reach subscribers every Monday 08:00 KST with nobody touching it, re-runs to be safe, and a report of every outcome, **so that** I never have to press send and never double-mail or late-mail a reader.

<objective>

Then the queue-safe concurrency and the tip checkouts that make every later re-run see the committed state.

Coordination: Phase 4.2 (sending via Resend on modaklog.characterplanner.co.kr) lands before this plan runs and owns the connection code in newsletter/delivery/send.py (host, port, login, From/Reply-To) and the delivery credential env names in the Deliver step. </objective>
